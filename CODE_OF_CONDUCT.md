# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, evidence-focused, and mindful that medical-AI work can affect people.
Critique methods and claims, not individuals. Do not post private, identifying, or
clinically sensitive information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate
misrepresentation of evidence are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to
protect contributors, research integrity, or privacy. Report conduct concerns through
the private channel listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
